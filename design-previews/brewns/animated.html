// How it is made

Our Bilona method

Five unhurried steps from fresh milk to sealed jar. The long way round — curd first, never a cream separator.

01

Fresh A2 milk

Milked daily from our own well-cared-for Gir cows at the gaushala.

02

Set into curd

Naturally fermented overnight using traditional methods — no shortcuts.

03

Churned to makkhan

The whole set curd is churned until pure makkhan separates out.

04

Cooked to gold

Simmered in a steam-jacketed kettle, so nothing catches or scorches.

05

Filled & sealed

Machine-filled into premium jars — less handling, better hygiene.
