How it is made

Our Bilona method

Five unhurried steps from fresh milk to sealed jar. The long way round — curd first, never a cream separator — with food-grade machinery doing the churning, cooking and filling. Scroll to follow it.

  1. 01 · Milk

    Fresh Gir A2A2 milk

    Milked daily from our own well-cared-for Gir cows at the gaushala.

  2. 02 · Curd

    Set into curd

    Naturally fermented overnight using traditional methods — no shortcuts.

  3. 03 · Bilona

    Churned to makkhan

    The whole set curd is churned — by machine now, which keeps every batch even — until pure makkhan separates out.

  4. 04 · Cooking

    Cooked to gold

    Makkhan is simmered in a steam-jacketed kettle, which holds an even heat so nothing catches or scorches.

  5. 05 · Sealed

    Filled and sealed

    Machine-filled and sealed into premium jars — less handling, better hygiene — and reserved for our Gir Gold Club families.
